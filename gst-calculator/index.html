Quick answer

The GST is Canada's 5% federal sales tax. To add it, multiply by 1.05: $100 becomes $105.00. To remove it, divide by 1.05: $105 is $100 before tax. In Alberta, Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut, the GST is the only sales tax.

GST formula

GST = price before tax × 0.05 Total with GST = price before tax × 1.05 Price before tax = total ÷ 1.05 GST included in a total = total × 5 ÷ 105 (≈ 4.762%)

Why not subtract 5%?

The GST was added on the price before tax, so remove it by dividing. On $105, subtracting 5% gives $99.75, which is 25 cents too low. Dividing by 1.05 gives the correct $100.00.

GST in Alberta and the territories

Alberta, Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut have no provincial sales tax: the 5% GST is all you pay. That makes Alberta the lowest sales tax in Canada, tied with the territories. Pick your province in the calculator above to see the full tax elsewhere.

GST vs HST vs PST

  • GST (5%): the federal tax, charged across Canada.
  • HST: the GST and the provincial tax combined into one rate in Ontario (13%), Nova Scotia (14%), and New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and PEI (15%). Use the HST calculator.
  • PST / RST: a separate provincial tax added to the GST in British Columbia (7%), Saskatchewan (6%) and Manitoba (7%). Use the GST PST calculator.
  • QST (9.975%): Quebec's provincial tax, added to the GST for 14.975% in total. Use the Quebec GST QST calculator.

GST registration for small businesses

A business must register for the GST/HST once its taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters (the small supplier threshold). Below that, registration is optional.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate GST?

Multiply the price before tax by 0.05 for the GST, or by 1.05 for the total. Example: $80 × 1.05 = $84.00, including $4.00 of GST.

How do you calculate GST backwards?

Divide the GST-included price by 1.05. Example: $105 ÷ 1.05 = $100 before tax. The GST inside any total is 4.762% of it (5 ÷ 105), not 5%.

What is the GST on $100?

$5.00, for a total of $105.00.

Is there PST in Alberta?

No. Alberta has no provincial sales tax, so only the 5% GST applies. The same goes for Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut.

Is GST 5% in every province?

Yes. The federal GST is 5% everywhere in Canada. In Ontario and the Atlantic provinces it is part of the HST; in British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba a separate PST or RST is added; in Quebec the QST is added.

What is not charged GST?

Basic groceries, prescription drugs, medical devices and most residential rent are zero-rated or exempt. Restaurant meals, snacks, soft drinks and alcohol are taxable.

Is the GST holiday still in effect?

No. The temporary GST/HST break on certain items ran from December 14, 2024 to February 15, 2025. The normal 5% GST applies again.
