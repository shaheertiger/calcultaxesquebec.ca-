### Calculator rounding (shared `assets/js/calculator.js`, remove mode only)
The live calculator computed TPS and TVQ from the *unrounded* amount before tax. The static tables
used the *rounded* amount. They disagreed by a cent on about 5% of totals (e.g. 0,06 $: TVQ 0,01 vs 0,00).
Remove mode now rounds the amount before tax first, so the three numbers on screen agree with
"TPS = avant taxes × 5 %". Add mode (the homepage default) is untouched. Service-worker cache bumped to `ctq-v12`.
