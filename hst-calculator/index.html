Quick answer

The HST is 13% in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia and 15% in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and PEI. To add it, multiply by 1 + the rate: $100 × 1.13 = $113.00. To remove it, divide: $113 ÷ 1.13 = $100 before tax.

HST formula

HST = price before tax × rate Total with HST = price before tax × (1 + rate) Price before tax = total ÷ (1 + rate) HST included = total × rate ÷ (1 + rate)
  • 13% HST included = total × 13 ÷ 113 (≈ 11.504%)
  • 14% HST included = total × 14 ÷ 114 (≈ 12.281%)
  • 15% HST included = total × 15 ÷ 115 (≈ 13.043%)

Why not subtract 13%?

The HST was added on the price before tax, so remove it by dividing. On $113, subtracting 13% gives $98.31, which is $1.69 too low. Dividing by 1.13 gives the correct $100.00.

HST vs GST

The HST is the 5% federal GST and the provincial sales tax charged as one tax: in Ontario, 5% + 8% = 13%; in Nova Scotia, 5% + 9% = 14%; elsewhere, 5% + 10% = 15%. Alberta and the territories charge only the GST: see the GST calculator. For Ontario's point-of-sale rebates and HST tables, see the Ontario sales tax calculator.

Nova Scotia HST change

Nova Scotia cut its HST from 15% to 14% on April 1, 2025. On $1,000, that's $1,140.00 instead of $1,150.00.

Provinces without HST

British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba add a separate PST or RST to the GST (see the GST PST calculator), and Quebec adds the QST (14.975% in total, see the Quebec GST QST calculator). The Canada sales tax calculator covers every province.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate HST?

Multiply the price before tax by the HST rate: 0.13 in Ontario, 0.14 in Nova Scotia, 0.15 in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and PEI. For the total, multiply by 1.13, 1.14 or 1.15.

How do you calculate HST backwards?

Divide the HST-included price by 1 + the rate. Example in Ontario: $113 ÷ 1.13 = $100 before tax. The HST inside a total is 11.504% of it at 13%, 12.281% at 14% and 13.043% at 15%.

Which provinces charge HST?

Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and Labrador. The other provinces charge the GST alone or the GST plus a separate provincial tax.

What is the HST in Nova Scotia?

14% since April 1, 2025, down from 15%. $100 before tax = $114.00.

What is the difference between HST and GST?

The GST is the 5% federal tax. The HST combines the GST with the provincial sales tax into a single rate, so in Ontario the 13% HST is 5% federal plus 8% provincial.

Is there HST in Quebec?

No. Quebec charges the 5% GST and its own 9.975% QST separately, for 14.975% in total. Use the Quebec GST QST calculator.

What is HST on $100?

$13.00 in Ontario (total $113.00), $14.00 in Nova Scotia (total $114.00) and $15.00 in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and PEI (total $115.00).
