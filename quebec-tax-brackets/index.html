Quebec Tax Brackets 2026

Quebec and federal income tax brackets for 2026, the combined marginal rate a Quebec resident actually pays, and the real tax on common salaries.

Quick answer

Quebec has 4 tax brackets in 2026: 14% up to $54,345, 19% up to $108,680, 24% up to $132,245 and 25.75% above. With federal tax, combined marginal rates run from 25.69% to 53.31%. On $60,000, total income tax is about $9,546 (15.9%).

Example: how the brackets apply to $60,000

  • First $54,345 × 14% = $7,608.30
  • Remaining $5,655 × 19% = $1,074.45
  • Quebec tax before credits = $8,682.75
  • Less non-refundable credits ($18,952 basic personal amount, QPP, EI, QPIP at 14%) and the worker deduction: about $5,118 of Quebec tax actually paid.

So even though your last dollar is taxed at 19% in Quebec, your average rate is far lower. For your own salary, use the calculator above or the Quebec income tax calculator.

Brackets and RRSP savings

Brackets apply to taxable income, after deductions such as RRSP contributions. A $1,000 RRSP contribution at $60,000 of income saves about $361.18 in tax, your marginal rate. Sources: Revenu Québec and Canada Revenue Agency, 2026 parameters.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Quebec tax brackets for 2026?

14% up to $54,345, 19% from $54,345 to $108,680, 24% from $108,680 to $132,245 and 25.75% above. Federal tax is added on top, reduced by 16.5% for Quebec residents.

What is the marginal tax rate on $60,000 in Quebec?

36.12% in 2026: 17.12% federal (after the abatement) plus 19% Quebec. That rate applies to your next dollar, not your whole income.

What is the highest tax bracket in Quebec?

25.75% on Quebec taxable income over $132,245. Combined with the top federal rate of 33% (less the abatement), the top marginal rate is 53.31% above $258,482.

Does moving into a higher bracket reduce my take-home pay?

No. Only the dollars above the threshold are taxed at the higher rate, so a raise always leaves you with more money after tax.

How much income is tax-free in Quebec?

Thanks to the basic personal amount ($18,952 Quebec, $16,452 federal) and contribution credits, an employee pays no Quebec income tax until about $21,750 of salary and no federal tax until about $19,300.

Why is federal tax lower for Quebec residents?

Quebec residents get a 16.5% abatement on basic federal tax because Quebec funds some programs itself. The lowest federal bracket is effectively 11.69% instead of 14%.
