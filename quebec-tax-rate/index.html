Quick answer

The Quebec sales tax rate is 14.975%: 5% GST (federal) + 9.975% QST (provincial). Multiply a price by 1.14975 to get the total: $100 before tax = $114.98. Rates are unchanged in 2026.

GST 5% QST 9.975% Combined 14.975%

In Quebec, the GST is 5% and the QST is 9.975%. The combined total is generally 14.975% on taxable goods and services. The QST is calculated on the selling price before taxes, not on the GST.

How the Quebec tax rate is applied

Both taxes are calculated on the price before tax: the QST is not charged on the GST. Each tax is rounded to the cent, which is why $100 gives $114.98, not $114.975. To take tax out of a total, divide by 1.14975 with the reverse tax calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax in Quebec?

14.975% in total: the 5% federal GST plus the 9.975% Quebec Sales Tax (QST), both calculated on the price before tax.

How much is tax on $100 in Quebec?

$14.98: $5.00 GST plus $9.98 QST, for a total of $114.98.

Is Quebec tax higher than Ontario?

Yes. Quebec's combined 14.975% is about 2 points higher than Ontario's 13% HST. On $100, you pay $114.98 in Quebec and $113.00 in Ontario.

Does Quebec have HST?

No. Quebec charges two separate taxes, the GST (5%) and the QST (9.975%). HST is used in Ontario and the Atlantic provinces.

What is the Quebec income tax rate?

Quebec income tax runs from 14% to 25.75% in 2026, on top of federal tax. See the 2026 Quebec tax brackets.
