Quick answer

To take tax out of a Quebec price, divide the total by 1.14975. Example: $114.98 ÷ 1.14975 = $100.00 before tax, which includes $5.00 GST and $9.98 QST. About 13% of any tax-included Quebec price is tax.

GST 5% QST 9.975% Combined 14.975%

In Quebec, the GST is 5% and the QST is 9.975%. The combined total is generally 14.975% on taxable goods and services. The QST is calculated on the selling price before taxes, not on the GST.

Reverse vs forward: which do you need?

Use reverse when you have a receipt or a price that already includes tax and need the amount before tax, for an expense report, a bookkeeping entry or an input tax credit claim. To add tax to a price, use the Quebec sales tax calculator.

Why not just subtract 14.975%?

Tax was added on the price before tax, so it must be removed by dividing. On $114.98, subtracting 14.975% gives $97.76, which is $2.24 too low. Dividing by 1.14975 gives the correct $100.00.

Frequently asked questions

How do you reverse calculate Quebec sales tax?

Divide the tax-included price by 1.14975. The result is the price before tax; 5% of it is the GST and 9.975% of it is the QST.

What is the price before tax of $50 in Quebec?

$50 ÷ 1.14975 = $43.49 before tax, with $2.17 of GST and $4.34 of QST included.

What percentage of a Quebec price is tax?

About 13.02% of a tax-included price is tax (14.975 ÷ 114.975). Taking 14.975% off the total is a common mistake that gives too low a number.

Why is my reverse calculation off by a cent?

Each tax is rounded to the cent on the original bill, so working backwards can differ by a cent or two from the shelf price.

Does the reverse calculator work for HST provinces?

No. It uses Quebec's GST (5%) and QST (9.975%). HST provinces use one harmonized rate, for example 13% in Ontario.
