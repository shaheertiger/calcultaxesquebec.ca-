Quick answer

British Columbia: 5% GST + 7% PST = 12%. Saskatchewan: 5% + 6% PST = 11%. Manitoba: 5% + 7% RST = 12%. Both taxes are calculated on the price before tax: $100 in BC = $5.00 GST + $7.00 PST = $112.00.

GST PST formula

GST = price before tax × 0.05 PST = price before tax × PST rate Total = price before tax + GST + PST Price before tax = total ÷ (1 + 0.05 + PST rate)

The PST is not charged on the GST: both are calculated on the same price before tax. Each tax is rounded to the cent, which is why a reverse calculation can be a cent off.

Why not subtract 12%?

The taxes were added on the price before tax, so remove them by dividing. On $112, subtracting 12% gives $98.56, which is $1.44 too low. Dividing by 1.12 gives the correct $100.00.

PST in British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba

  • British Columbia: 7% PST, administered by the BC government, charged on most goods and some services. Liquor is taxed at 10% and higher-priced passenger vehicles at higher rates.
  • Saskatchewan: 6% PST, charged on most goods and many services.
  • Manitoba: 7% RST (Retail Sales Tax), Manitoba's name for its PST.

Other provinces

Alberta and the territories charge only the 5% GST: see the GST calculator. Ontario and the Atlantic provinces combine both into one HST: see the HST calculator. Quebec works like the PST provinces with its own 9.975% QST: see the Quebec GST QST calculator. The Canada sales tax calculator covers every province.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate GST and PST?

Multiply the price before tax by 5% for the GST and by the provincial rate for the PST, then add both. In British Columbia: $100 × 5% = $5.00 GST, $100 × 7% = $7.00 PST, total $112.00.

Is PST charged on GST?

No. In British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba, the PST (RST in Manitoba) is calculated on the price before tax, not on the GST, so the two rates simply add up.

How do you remove GST and PST from a total?

Divide the tax-included price by 1 + the combined rate: 1.12 in BC and Manitoba, 1.11 in Saskatchewan. Example: $112 ÷ 1.12 = $100 before tax, including $5.00 GST and $7.00 PST.

What is the GST and PST in BC?

5% GST plus 7% PST, 12% in total. Some items carry a different PST rate, such as liquor (10%) and higher-priced passenger vehicles.

What is the sales tax in Saskatchewan?

5% GST plus 6% PST, 11% in total. $100 before tax = $111.00.

What is RST in Manitoba?

RST is Manitoba's Retail Sales Tax, its name for the PST: 7%. With the 5% GST, the total is 12%.

What is not charged PST?

Many basics are exempt from PST, such as basic groceries, prescription drugs and children's clothing, but the rules differ by province. Check with the provincial finance ministry for a specific item.
