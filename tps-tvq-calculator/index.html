Quick answer

TPS is Quebec's French name for the 5% GST; TVQ is the 9.975% QST. Both apply to the price before tax, for 14.975% in total: multiply by 1.14975, so $100 becomes $114.98. To remove them, divide by 1.14975.

GST 5% QST 9.975% Combined 14.975%

In Quebec, the GST is 5% and the QST is 9.975%. The combined total is generally 14.975% on taxable goods and services. The QST is calculated on the selling price before taxes, not on the GST.

Frequently asked questions

What does TPS TVQ mean?

TPS (taxe sur les produits et services) is the French name of the 5% federal GST. TVQ (taxe de vente du Québec) is the 9.975% Quebec Sales Tax, or QST. Together they add 14.975%.

How do I calculate TPS and TVQ?

Multiply the price before tax by 0.05 for the TPS and by 0.09975 for the TVQ, both on the same price. Or multiply by 1.14975 for the total: $100 becomes $114.98.

Is TVQ calculated on TPS?

No. Since 2013 the TVQ is calculated on the price before tax, not on the price plus TPS.

What does taxes en sus mean?

Plus tax: the price shown does not include TPS and TVQ. Taxes incluses means tax included.

How do I remove TPS and TVQ from a price?

Divide the tax-included price by 1.14975. $114.98 ÷ 1.14975 = $100.00 before tax.
