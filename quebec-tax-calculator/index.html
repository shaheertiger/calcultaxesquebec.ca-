Quick answer

How to calculate Quebec sales tax

To add tax, multiply the amount before tax by 5% for the GST and by 9.975% for the QST, then add both to the original amount. To remove tax, divide the tax-included total by 1.14975 to find the amount before tax.

Example on $100

On $100.00: the GST is $5.00, the QST is $9.98, and the total with tax is $114.98.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax in Quebec?

In Quebec, the GST is 5% and the QST is 9.975%, for a combined rate of generally 14.975% on taxable goods and services.

How do I calculate GST and QST in Quebec?

Multiply the amount before tax by 5% for the GST, then by 9.975% for the QST. Add the amount before tax, the GST and the QST together to get the total.

How do I remove tax from a price in Quebec?

Divide the tax-included price by 1.14975 to get the approximate amount before tax. The calculator then shows the GST and QST separately.

Is the QST calculated on the GST?

No. The QST is calculated on the selling price before taxes, not on the amount including the GST.

How much is tax on $100 in Quebec?

On $100 before tax, the GST is $5.00 and the QST is $9.98, so you pay $14.98 in tax and $114.98 in total.

Is Quebec sales tax 15%?

Almost: the combined rate is 14.975%, not 15%. Multiply the price by 1.14975 to get the total.

What is not taxed in Quebec?

Basic groceries, prescription drugs, most residential rent and some health and education services are zero-rated or exempt. Prepared meals, alcohol, soft drinks and snacks are taxable.
