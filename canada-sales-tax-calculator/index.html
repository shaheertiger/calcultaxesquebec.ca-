GST, HST, PST, QST: what's the difference?

  • GST (5%): the federal tax, and the only one in Alberta and the territories.
  • HST: GST and provincial tax merged into one (Ontario and the Atlantic provinces).
  • PST / RST: a separate provincial tax in British Columbia (7%), Saskatchewan (6%) and Manitoba (7%).
  • QST (9.975%): the Quebec Sales Tax, collected by Revenu Québec. See the Quebec sales tax calculator.

How to calculate sales tax in each province

Multiply the price before tax by 1 + the total rate: × 1.13 in Ontario, × 1.15 in New Brunswick, × 1.12 in British Columbia, × 1.05 in Alberta. To remove tax from a total, divide by the same number, as in the reverse tax calculator. Each tax is rounded to the cent.

Rates in force in 2026 per the Canada Revenue Agency and provincial finance ministries. Nova Scotia's HST dropped from 15% to 14% on April 1, 2025. Some items are zero-rated or exempt (basic groceries, for example).

Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax rate in Canada?

The 5% federal GST applies everywhere. Depending on the province, a provincial tax (PST, RST or QST) is added, or the GST is replaced by a single HST. Totals range from 5% (Alberta and the territories) to 15% (New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island).

Which provinces have HST?

Ontario (13%), Nova Scotia (14%), New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and Prince Edward Island (15%). HST combines the GST and the provincial portion into one tax.

How do you calculate sales tax in Canada?

Multiply the price before tax by the province's total rate: 1.13 in Ontario, 1.12 in British Columbia, 1.14975 in Quebec, 1.05 in Alberta. To remove tax, divide the total by the same number.

Which province has the lowest sales tax?

Alberta, Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut charge only the 5% GST. Quebec (14.975%) and the 15% HST provinces are the highest.

Is PST charged on top of GST?

No. In provinces with PST, RST or QST, the provincial tax is calculated on the price before tax, just like the GST, so the two rates simply add up.

Which tax applies when I buy online from another province?

Generally the tax of the province where the item is delivered. An order shipped to Ontario carries 13% HST even if the seller is in Quebec.
