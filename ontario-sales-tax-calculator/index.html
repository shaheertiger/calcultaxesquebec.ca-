Quick answer

Ontario's sales tax is the 13% HST (5% federal + 8% provincial). Multiply by 1.13 for the total: $100 = $113.00. To remove HST, divide by 1.13. That's about 2 points less than Quebec (14.975%).

Ontario point-of-sale rebate

On some items Ontario doesn't charge its 8% portion, so only 5% applies: children's clothing and footwear, diapers, children's car seats, printed books, newspapers, and prepared food and drinks of $4 or less. Basic groceries and prescription drugs are not taxed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sales tax in Ontario?

13% HST (Harmonized Sales Tax): 5% federal and 8% provincial, charged as a single tax.

How do you calculate HST in Ontario?

Multiply the price before tax by 0.13 for the HST, or by 1.13 for the total. Example: $50 × 1.13 = $56.50.

How do you remove HST from a price in Ontario?

Divide the tax-included price by 1.13. Example: $113 ÷ 1.13 = $100 before tax, including $13 of HST.

Are taxes lower in Ontario than in Quebec?

Yes, by about 2 points: 13% versus 14.975%. On a $1,000 purchase you pay $130.00 of tax in Ontario and $149.75 in Quebec.

Which items get an HST rebate in Ontario?

Ontario rebates its 8% portion at the till on children's clothing and footwear, diapers, children's car seats, printed books and meals of $4 or less, so only 5% applies. Basic groceries are zero-rated.
